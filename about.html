About

A neuroimaging scientist focused on human brain chemistry

Mark Mikkelsen is a neuroimaging scientist specializing in magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) research. He earned his Ph.D. in neuroimaging at Cardiff University, and most recently served as Assistant Professor of Neuroimaging in Radiology at Weill Cornell Medicine from August 2022 to April 2026.

Biography

Methods, collaboration, and open science

My research develops novel MRS methods and applies them to questions in neurological and neuropsychiatric research. A particular focus is the noninvasive measurement of low-concentration metabolites in the living human brain, including the inhibitory neurotransmitter γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA) and the antioxidant glutathione.

I earned my doctorate in neuroimaging at the Cardiff University Brain Research Imaging Centre (CUBRIC). My subsequent postdoctoral training included work at Royal Holloway, University of London and the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine before joining Weill Cornell Medicine, where I later held a faculty appointment as an Assistant Professor of Neuroimaging in Radiology.

Throughout my career, my overarching goal has been to make MRS measurements more reliable and useful: through methods development, multi-site studies, open-source software, and shared data standards.
